The History

Medivial times

The earliest known times of soccer were in the medival times.
Whole entire villages would chase an inflated pig bladder.
They would chase it over miles of open space.
They didn't have many rules so it led to many fights and brawls within the villages.

Birth of modern soccer

The people began developing rules to avoid major conflicts.
One of the major rule changes was that you couldn't use your hands.
This rule officialy split soccer away from rugby.
English public schools began playing their own rules.

Global expansion

British workers carried the rules throughout the world which led soccer to spread rapidly.
The Federation Interationale de Football Association (FIFA) was founded in 1904 in France.
This led to the first world cup in 1930 which took place in Uruguay.

Soccer today

With the advancement of technology, FIFA has been implenting new changes.
FIFA has used new technology like the Video Assistance Referee (VAR) which helps the on field referee make more accurate calls.
FIFA also added advanced chips inside of the balls to see whether the ball crossed the goal line or not.
FIFA has continued to add new technology to make it easier to officiate.
